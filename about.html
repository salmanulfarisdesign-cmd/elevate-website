The Founder

Hi, I'm Salman.

I'm 21 years old, based in Kerala. I started Elevate to help local business owners use AI tools to automate their internal tasks — saving time and reducing the manual work that slows businesses down.

I'm self-educated in digital marketing and AI automation. I learned by doing — building systems, studying how businesses operate, and applying that knowledge to real problems. What I know, I learned from practice.

My focus is straightforward: help business owners in Kerala and the Gulf automate repetitive tasks, manage their digital presence, and understand exactly what's happening in their business at every step.
