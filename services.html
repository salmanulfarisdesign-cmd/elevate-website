FAQ

Questions local business owners ask us.

Do I need to understand technology to work with you?
Not at all. Everything we do is explained in plain language. You don't need to know anything about digital marketing or AI tools — that's our job. We teach you what's relevant to you, at your pace.
How long before I see results?
It depends on the service and your starting point. Social media management typically shows engagement growth within 4–8 weeks. SEO takes longer — 3 to 6 months to see meaningful ranking changes. We'll always give you an honest timeline before we start — never inflated promises to close the deal.
Do I have to sign a long-term contract?
No. We don't lock you into long contracts. We believe we should earn your business every month. If you're not satisfied with what we're doing, you can stop. We'd rather build a relationship on trust than on paperwork.
I've worked with agencies before and felt cheated. Why is Elevate different?
We understand the concern — many businesses have had disappointing experiences with agencies. The honest answer is: we're just starting out, which means we don't have a long track record yet. We don't have big numbers to show. What we do have is a non-negotiable commitment to delivering what we say we will. You can see exactly where we stand on our portfolio and testimonials pages — if it's empty, it means we haven't completed enough client work yet.
Can I hire you for just one service, or do I need a package?
You can hire us for any single service that fits your business right now. There are no minimum packages. Many clients start with one service — like social media management — and add others as they see results and trust builds. We work at your pace.
